# Glossary: what things are called in the interface

This page gives one name for each thing a teacher or a student sees in LeaRN. Use it for every label, heading, button, message, email and help page. If a word is not here, add a row before you write it in two places.

Interface words and code names differ on purpose. The interface uses words a teacher knows from Kahoot or Socrative. Routes, tables, TypeScript types and the `learn.v1` transfer format keep the names they have (`item_banks`, `items`, `/author`, `sessions`), so a rename story changes copy and nothing else. "Item" stays the word inside `src/lib/ngn` and `docs/01-NGN-ITEM-SPEC.md`, where it is the NCLEX term for a question.

Every row is an owner decision, made on 2026-10-13 in three rounds (issue #410, then two reviews of this page). The rename stories (#412 to #416) apply all of them.

Terms are written in sentence case in the interface: "Start a live session", not "Start a Live Session".

What follows from these, with no further decision needed:

- "Item 3 of 10" becomes "Question 3 of 10" (`src/lib/live/positionLabel.ts`).
- "Instructor-paced" becomes "Teacher-paced"; "Student-paced" stays (`src/app/author/banks/[bankId]/page.tsx`).
- "Your instructor" becomes "your teacher" in student copy (`src/components/live/StudentRoom.tsx`, `src/components/classes/JoinByCodeForm.tsx`).
- "Go to your item banks" becomes "Go to your quizzes" (`src/lib/auth/landing.ts`).
- "Word bank" stays. It is the NCLEX name for the list of choices in a drag-and-drop question (`src/lib/authoring/issueMessages.ts`), and it is the one place "bank" still appears.
- "Your LeaRN administrator" stays, as an earlier owner decision, for the person who adds teachers to the shared workspace. No screen uses the phrase today; this page reserves it.

## Words we do not use

In the interface, that is. Most of these are fine in code and comments.

- **Bank**, **item bank**, **question bank**: say "quiz". "Word bank" is the one exception.
- **Item**: say "question". It stays in `src/lib/ngn` and the item spec.
- **Room**, **session console**, **console**, **host**: say "live session", or "in this session".
- **Session** alone, and **session code**: say "live session" and "join code".
- **Instructor**, **author**, **authoring**, **the author home**: say "teacher" and "library".
- **Member**, **founder**, **organization**, **org**: say "teacher", "colleague", "the person who started this workspace", "workspace".
- **CJMM**: say "clinical judgment step".
- **EHR**, **chart**, **record** alone, **section** of a patient record: say "patient record" and "tab".
- **Unfiled**, **All content**: say "Not in a folder" and "All quizzes".
- **Take-home work**, **graded work**, **homework**, **work**: say "assignment".
- **Practice bank**, **practice run**: say "practice quiz", or just "practice".
- **Launch**, **Run**, **Make**: say "Start" for a live session, and "New" then "Create" for everything else.
- **Delete** for a quiz, a question, a case study or a class: say "Archive".
- **Paywall**, **plan**, **plan tier**, **upgrade**, **seat**, **license**: say "subscription", "free trial" and "Billing". There is one price for the workspace, not per teacher.
- **Comp**, **promo**, **coupon**, **promotion code**: there are none. Beta access is named only on its own page.
