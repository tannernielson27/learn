# Fluid, Electrolyte and Oxygenation Practice

Fictional content written for the LeaRN test corpus (#339). No real patients.

The answers are at the end of this document.

## 1.

A client with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) is short of breath. The oxygen saturation is 86% on room air.

Complete the sentence by choosing from the lists of options.

The nurse should first \_\_\_1\_\_\_ and then \_\_\_2\_\_\_.

- Options for 1: sit the client upright | give oxygen at 15 L/min by non-rebreather mask | call the rapid response team
- Options for 2: start oxygen at 1 to 2 L/min by nasal cannula | encourage the client to lie flat | give a sedative for anxiety

## 2.

A client has been vomiting for 2 days. The serum potassium is 2.9 mEq/L.

Complete the sentence by choosing from the lists of options.

The client is at highest risk for \_\_\_1\_\_\_ as evidenced by \_\_\_2\_\_\_.

- Options for 1: seizures | cardiac dysrhythmias | hypertension
- Options for 2: a serum potassium of 2.9 mEq/L | the client's age | the length of the illness

Scoring Rule: Rationale

## 5.

The nurse is caring for a client with a serum sodium of 128 mEq/L. Which actions should the nurse take? Select all that apply.

A. Restrict free water intake as prescribed
B. Monitor the client's level of consciousness
C. Encourage the client to drink 3 L of water a day
D. Start seizure precautions
E. Give a hypotonic IV solution

## Answer Key

1. sit the client upright; start oxygen at 1 to 2 L/min by nasal cannula
2. cardiac dysrhythmias; a serum potassium of 2.9 mEq/L
3. hyperkalemia; peaked T waves
4. hypocalcemia; tingling around the mouth
5. A, B, D
