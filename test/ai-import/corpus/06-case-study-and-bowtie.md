# Unfolding Case Study: Pneumonia With Worsening Condition

Fictional content written for the LeaRN test corpus (#339). No real patients.

Learning objective: recognize early sepsis in an older adult with pneumonia.

Patient: Walter Fenwick, 72 years old, male. Setting: Medical unit.
