## Question 1

A client who had abdominal surgery 6 hours ago reports pain of 8 on a scale of 0 to 10. Which action should the nurse take first?

A. Reposition the client and reassess in 1 hour
B. *Assess the incision site and the client's vital signs
C. Encourage the client to use the incentive spirometer
D. Document the pain rating in the client's record

Rationale: Assessment comes before intervention. New, severe pain after surgery can signal a complication, so the nurse assesses the incision and vital signs before choosing a comfort measure.

## Question 3

A client with type 1 diabetes is found sweating and shaky. The capillary blood glucose is 52 mg/dL, and the client is awake and able to swallow. What should the nurse give first?

A. 4 ounces of fruit juice
B. A glass of whole milk with crackers
C. Glucagon 1 mg intramuscularly
D. Regular insulin as prescribed

✅ Answer: A

## Question 5

The nurse is preparing to insert a peripheral IV catheter. Place the steps in the order the nurse should perform them.

A. Apply the tourniquet above the selected site
B. Perform hand hygiene and put on clean gloves
C. Clean the site with chlorhexidine and let it dry
D. Insert the catheter with the bevel up
E. Release the tourniquet and connect the extension set

Correct order: B, A, C, D, E
